About

The editor behind the cut

I'm Mostafa Ali Mahrous, a video editor based in Giza, Egypt. I handle the full process: concept, script, edit, motion graphics and color grading — you send raw footage, you get back a finished ad.


I've worked with marketing agencies including Art Media and Cord Digital, plus a media production company. My work includes AI-generated ads for Saudi clients such as One Step and Al-Manarat, reels for the A.C.P Facebook page — one Burberry hair reel reached ~24.8K organic views — and product, perfume and podcast edits for Egyptian brands.
